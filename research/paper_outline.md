# Autonomous Workplace Agents

## Abstract

This research investigates secure deployment of AI-powered workplace agents capable of performing operational tasks including email triage, customer support, lead qualification, and reporting.

We evaluate AI agent behaviour under adversarial conditions and propose layered safety controls including policy enforcement, prompt injection detection, human approval workflows, audit logging, and risk scoring.

---

## Introduction

Large language models are increasingly used in operational environments.

As autonomy increases, new attack surfaces emerge:

- Prompt injection
- Tool misuse
- Workflow manipulation
- Data leakage
- Unintended actions

This project explores practical mitigations.

---

## Research Questions

1. Can workplace AI agents resist prompt injection attacks?

2. Which actions require human oversight?

3. How should AI agents be audited?

4. What level of autonomy is appropriate?
